Credit risk score

How we calculate the score

The analyzer produces a composite score from 0–1000 using six risk components. The assignment specifies the following dimensions, key metrics and weights.

Income Stability25%Monthly income by category, regularity, source diversity and growth trend.
Debt Service20%Identified EMI obligations, FOIR, EMI bounce rate and on-time payment rate.
Liquidity15%Average / minimum end-of-day balance and number of negative balance days.
Banking Behaviour10%Cheque / payment bounce count and charges, penalty fees and overdraft usage.
Fraud Indicators15%Statement integrity, circular transactions, overnight transactions and structuring patterns.
Expense Management15%Essential vs discretionary split, fixed vs variable ratio and month-on-month trends.
